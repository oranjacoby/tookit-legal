Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

TookIt helps you remember your pill and prove to yourself you took it. To do that we need a little information, and some of it is sensitive. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who it is shared with, and how you control it.

What we collect

• Account details: email and name, plus phone and gender if you choose to add them.

• Your pill: its name, reminder time, regimen and pack start date.

• Intake history: when you marked or verified a pill, and the proof photos you take.

• Technical data: a push-notification token, device type and app version.

• Crash reports: when the app hits an error, a technical report is sent (the error, device model and OS version), with no name, email, photos or pill details.

• Usage data: which screens and actions you use, to improve the app. It stays with us and is never given to advertising or analytics companies.

Health information

Information about your pill, your intake and your pack is sensitive. We use it only to run the app for you: reminders, verification, streaks and your expected period. Your expected period is calculated from your pack date only; we do not track ovulation or fertility. We will never use this information for advertising or sell it.

AI photo check

If you allow it, your proof photo is sent to Anthropic, an AI provider, for an automatic check that today's pill was taken. Only the photo and the technical details the check needs (the day in your pack and your reminder style) are sent, with no name, email or identifier. Anthropic processes the photo on our behalf, does not use it to train its models, and deletes it after a limited period under its commercial terms.

You can turn the check off anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security. Photos are then kept only as proof in your account, and you mark the pill yourself.

How we use it

We use your information to send your reminders, verify intake, keep your streak accurate, show your pack status and expected period, secure your account and improve TookIt. We never sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share data only with providers that help us run the app, and only what each one needs:

• Supabase: data storage and sign-in. Servers are in the EU (Frankfurt).

• Anthropic: checking proof photos, only if you allow it. Processing takes place in the United States.

• Sentry: receiving technical crash reports, with nothing that identifies you, so we can fix problems.

• Expo, Apple and Google: delivering notifications to your device.

• Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose to sign in with them. We do not sell data or pass it to advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, your account details, pills, intake history, proof photos and usage data are permanently deleted.

How we keep it safe

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is restricted per user, so each person sees only her own data.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, in the app or by contacting us. We honor requests under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, GDPR and CCPA.

Children's privacy

TookIt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
