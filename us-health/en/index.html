Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (US)

Last updated: 2026-11-13

This policy applies to residents of Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and other US states with consumer health data laws. It explains how TookIt handles your consumer health data and supplements our Privacy Policy.

Health data we collect

• Your contraceptive pill: its name, regimen, reminder time and pack start date.

• Intake history: when you took, marked, missed or snoozed a pill.

• Proof photos of your pill pack.

• Data we derive: your expected period, streaks and missed-pill status.

Where it comes from

Everything comes from you, through the app. Your expected period and streaks are calculated from what you enter. We do not buy health data or receive it from anyone else.

Why we use it

Only to provide the features you ask for: reminders, intake verification, streaks, pack status and your expected period. We never use it for advertising.

Who receives it

• Supabase (our processor): stores all of the data above for us.

• Anthropic (processor): receives a proof photo and your pack day, only if you consent, to check that today's pill was taken.

• Apple and Google: they send us your name and email when you sign in. We send them no health data.

• Sentry (crash reports): receives no health data.

We do not share health data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

No sale, no geofencing

We do not sell consumer health data. We do not use geofencing or collect your location.

Your rights

You can:

• confirm whether we hold your health data, and access it;

• get the list of every third party that received it, with contact details;

• withdraw consent;

• have it deleted, by us and by our processors.

Delete your account in Settings → Privacy & Security, or email tookit.support@gmail.com. We reply within 45 days. If needed, we may take another 45 days and will tell you why. We will never treat you differently for using these rights.

Appeals

If we turn down your request, email tookit.support@gmail.com with the subject "Appeal". We will answer in writing within 45 days. If you disagree with the outcome, you may contact your state Attorney General (Washington, Nevada or Connecticut).

Security

Only staff and processors who need the data can access it. It is encrypted in transit and at rest.
